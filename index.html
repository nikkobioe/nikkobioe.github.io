Hello there!

I am a postdoctoral research fellow in bioengineering at the University of British Columbia in Prof. Peter Zandstra's lab with a background in computational biophysics, biomaterials, synthetic biology, and immunoengineering. I received my PhD in Engineering Sciences from Harvard University in 2024, advised by Prof. Dave Mooney. My current research focuses on the systems pharmacology of T cell engagers and understanding childhood T cell leukemogenesis.

For more details on my work, please visit Research and Publications.

Research Overview

My research combines computational fluid dynamics, reference-map techniques, numerical linear algebra, and physical experiments to study complex fluid-structure interaction (FSI) systems.

Reference Map Method
Fluid-Structure Interaction

Eulerian Simulation of Soft Solids in Fluid Streams

Developing fully Eulerian numerical frameworks to simulate large-deformation viscoelastic solids immersed in incompressible viscous flows, enabling precise modeling of biological valves and flexible structures.
