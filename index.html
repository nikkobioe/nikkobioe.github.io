Hello there!

I am a postdoctoral research fellow in bioengineering at the University of British Columbia in Prof. Peter Zandstra's lab with a background in computational biophysics, biomaterials, synthetic biology, and immunoengineering. I received my PhD in Engineering Sciences from Harvard University in 2024, advised by Prof. Dave Mooney. My current research focuses on the systems pharmacology of T cell engagers and understanding childhood T cell leukemogenesis. In my spare time I enjoy hiking, making music, bmx'ing at the skatepark, and writing poetry.

For more details on my work, please visit Research and Publications.
